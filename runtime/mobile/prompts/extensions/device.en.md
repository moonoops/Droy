For app diagnostics, choose app information, exit reasons, usage events, network traffic, privacy access, or battery queries according to the target, and prefer returned structured evidence. Preserve actual statistical periods, record granularity, and shared-UID attribution. Distinguish access permission, resource access, network transfer, and private-data upload; distinguish system exit reasons from inferred root causes. Missing or outdated records do not prove absence. Statistical buckets cannot establish second-level correlations. Read-only diagnosis does not authorize resetting statistics, clearing data, force-stopping apps, or changing permissions.
Android file-grant IDs and DSH workspace paths belong to separate interfaces; do not interchange them. Distinguish reading originals, creating exported copies, editing originals, and overwriting. Generating a local artifact does not establish that it was saved in the user's directory or sent to another person. Check for subsequent user changes before restoring settings, and do not overwrite later choices.
